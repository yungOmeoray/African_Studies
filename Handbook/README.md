# Handbook Upload

Upload the exact handbook version for the upcoming INCOSE ASEP exam to this folder.

The study webpage will be created only after the handbook is provided. Its instructional content, explanations, and practice-question subject matter must be derived strictly from that uploaded version. External research may be used only to understand the general style and format of INCOSE ASEP exam questions, not as a source of study content.

## Planned study experience

Because the study window is two days and the handbook has already been read once, the webpage should prioritize:

- a focused two-day review plan;
- active recall rather than passive rereading;
- exam-style practice questions;
- immediate answer explanations with handbook references;
- weak-area tracking and targeted retry sessions;
- timed mixed-question practice; and
- a final rapid-review mode.
